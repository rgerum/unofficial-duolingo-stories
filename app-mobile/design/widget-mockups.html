Next Story Widget — design concepts

All layouts use only primitives available in expo-widgets / @expo/ui (stacks, images, shapes, progress, flat colors). Story icon comes from the Duolingo stories CDN, rasterized to PNG server-side. Example state: “Good Morning”, Danish, 23/279 stories done.

Concept A

Character Spotlight

Keeps the app’s card look. Course chip with flag, big story title, character icon on the right, slim green progress bar pinned to the bottom.

Safe default — works in dark and light, information-dense but calm.

Concept B

Progress Ring

Character sits inside a course-progress ring (SwiftUI Gauge). Fitness-app energy: the ring filling up is the hook to come back.

Medium adds a “Continue” pill in brand green — the whole widget is one tap target, the pill is just an affordance.

Concept C

Color Block

Full-bleed brand color with soft circle accents (ZStack + Circle shapes) and the character floating with a shadow. Loudest option — pops on any wallpaper.

Background color could rotate per story set, so the widget visibly changes as you progress.

Read / Listen Split
